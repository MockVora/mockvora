# MockVora

**AI Voice Mock Interview Platform** — practice real interviews with a voice-based AI interviewer.

🌐 Website: [mockvora.com](https://mockvora.com)
📱 Android app: [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mockora.app)

## What is MockVora?
MockVora helps job seekers, students and engineers prepare for technical,
behavioral and HR interviews with personalized questions and instant feedback.

## Features
- Real-time voice conversation with an AI interviewer
- Personalized follow-up questions based on your answers
- Technical topics: Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, AWS/GCP, Terraform
- Behavioral and HR round practice
